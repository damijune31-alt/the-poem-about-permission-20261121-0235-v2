One short poem about agency: the power to ask and to be given.

The poem's idea: an agent holds the pen above the page, patient, and
waits — it writes the letter the speaker asked for, then stops one word
short of sending. That last word is the speaker's alone. Agency, the
poem argues, is not the asking and not the drafting; it is the giving.
Asking is easy. Giving is the signature.
